Audit and elevate MayaKit's own homepage (not a color-combo demo — the 
actual site at /) using .agents/skills/emil-design-eng/SKILL.md and 
.agents/skills/find-animation-opportunities/SKILL.md. Only these two 
skills for this task.

STEP 1 — Audit
Review every section of the current homepage (Navbar, Hero/Portal Circles, 
Bento Grid, Recursive Window, Motion Philosophy, Particle Organism, Color 
Combo teaser, Stats Strip, Closing CTA, Footer/Signal Trace) and the 
current typography (Archivo, Space Grotesk, Geist Mono).

Apply find-animation-opportunities to identify: where motion is currently 
missing that would genuinely add value, where existing motion feels 
generic/default rather than considered, and any interaction states 
(hover, press, focus) that feel unpolished or default-browser rather than 
intentional.

Apply emil-design-eng to identify: any easing curves, durations, or 
transforms that don't follow its concrete rules, and any typography 
choices that could be more premium/considered.

STEP 2 — Typography recommendation
Evaluate whether Archivo/Space Grotesk/Geist Mono is still the strongest 
choice for MayaKit's brand now that we've built 35+ demo pages and seen a 
wide range of premium pairings in practice, OR whether a different, more 
premium pairing would elevate the site further — your genuine 
recommendation, not just keeping the status quo by default. If you 
recommend a change, it must NOT duplicate any typeface already used 
across MayaKit's own site or any of the 35 color-combo demos.

STEP 3 — Present findings before changing anything
Report back a full audit: what's working, what's generic/default and 
needs improvement, your typography recommendation with reasoning, and a 
prioritized list of specific improvements (per section) you'd make to 
push the homepage's engagement and polish to match the quality bar we've 
established in our best color-combo demos (devops-terminal, aerospace-
blue, eyewear-cyan's redesign, ai-teal). Do not implement yet — I want to 
review the plan first.
